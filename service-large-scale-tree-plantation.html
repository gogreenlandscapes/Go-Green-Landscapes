Large Scale Tree Plantation

Go Green Landscapes specializes in large-scale tree plantation projects for housing societies, commercial developments, parks, institutions, and government or private sector landscapes. Our goal is to promote greener, healthier, and more sustainable environments through professional plantation services.

We manage the complete plantation process — from site analysis and species selection to plantation execution and long-term maintenance. Every project is carefully planned to ensure maximum survival rate, healthy growth, and long-term environmental impact.

  • Roadside & Avenue Plantation
  • Commercial Project Landscaping
  • Institutional Plantation Projects
  • Corporate Campus Green Development
  • Government & NGO Plantation Drives
  • Orchard & Agricultural Tree Plantation

Complete Plantation Process

  • Site Survey & Soil Analysis
  • Selection of Suitable Tree Species
  • Landscape & Plantation Planning
  • Soil Preparation & Ground Treatment
  • Proper Spacing & Layout Design
  • Professional Tree Plantation
  • Support Systems (Staking & Protection)
  • Watering & Fertilization Setup

6-Month Maintenance & Care Program

We provide complete post-plantation care for up to 6 months to ensure healthy establishment of trees. Our maintenance program includes:

  • Regular watering schedules
  • Fertilization & soil enrichment
  • Pest & disease control
  • Pruning & growth management
  • Health monitoring of all plants
  • Replacement of failed plants during maintenance period
