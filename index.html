What areas does Go Green Landscapes serve?

We primarily serve Islamabad and surrounding areas including Rawalpindi, DHA phases, Bahria Town, housing societies along Kahuta Road, and other communities in the twin cities region.

Do you offer free landscaping consultations?

Yes! We offer an initial consultation to assess your space and discuss your vision. Contact us or WhatsApp us at +92 306 8145492 to schedule your free site visit.

How long does a typical landscaping project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope. A small residential garden may take 1–2 weeks, while large commercial projects can take several months. We provide a detailed timeline after the initial site assessment.

Do you provide eco-friendly and sustainable landscaping solutions?

Absolutely. We specialize in sustainable landscaping including drip irrigation systems to minimize water usage, native plant selection, organic soil enrichment, and eco-friendly materials that support local biodiversity.
